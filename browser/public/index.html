Allow location to browse

Location permission is required before opening websites in Musab X. Websites you open in this live view can then access your location when they request it.

Musab X does not save your coordinates. Camera and microphone access still require your browser approval. Permissions apply to websites in this live view. Location access does not change the relay’s IP address or guarantee a website’s region check will pass.
